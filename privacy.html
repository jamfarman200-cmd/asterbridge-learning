Privacy notice

Asterbridge Learning collects only information submitted voluntarily through its enquiry form, such as name, email, province, grade, subject, selected program and message, to respond to requests and coordinate tutoring.

Website form submissions, when connected, are stored in a Google Sheet controlled by the academy and may generate email notifications. Information is not sold. Access should be restricted to the academy operator and retained only as long as reasonably necessary for enquiry and service administration.

Do not submit sensitive information, passwords, financial details, health information or confidential school records. For students under 18, a parent or guardian should submit or supervise the enquiry. A parent/guardian should be involved in scheduling and communication.

Google may process data according to its own terms and privacy practices. Contact the academy to request correction or deletion of enquiry information. This template is not legal advice; review it for applicable Canadian privacy requirements before launch.
